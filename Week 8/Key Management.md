Key Management - is the set of techniques and procedures supporting the establishment and maintenance of keying relationships between authorised parties.

Key Management encompasses:
	Initialisation of users within a domain;
	Generation, distribution, and installation of keying material;
	Controlling the use of keying material;
	Update, revocation, and destruction of keying material; and
	Storage, backup/recovery, and archival of keying material

Key layering and Cryptoperiods:
	Master keys are keys at the highest level in the hierarchy - they are not encrypted,
	but are distributed manually and protected by procedural controls, and physical or electronic isolation.

Key-encrpyting keys are used for key transport or storage of other keys, and may themselves be encrypted.

Data keys are used for cryptographic operations on user data.

Cryptoperiod - is the time period of which a key may be validly used by legitimate parties.
