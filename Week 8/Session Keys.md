Key Establishment - is a process whereby a shared secret becomes available to two or more parties.

Key Transport - is a key establishment protocol where one party securely transfers its secret value to other parties.

Key Agreement - a protocol where a shared secret is derived from information contributed by each party, such that no party can predetermine the result.

Session key - Dynamic or session key establishment is a key agreement protocol where the key established by a group varies on a subsequent executions, and typically has a short lifetime.

 To limit available cipher text for cryptanalysis
